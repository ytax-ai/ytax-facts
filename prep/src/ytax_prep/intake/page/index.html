Your privacy

  • Your documents, your answers and the filled forms stay on this computer, in the folder you choose.
  • Nothing is sent to us. yTax has no server that receives your return, and nothing is collected: no account, no tracking, no usage data.
  • If you turn on the check by an AI model below, the page images go only to the model at the address that section shows.

      Notations at the top of Form 1040

      Form 1040 prints these above your name, and few returns carry any of them. They change no figure computed here; pay earned in a combat zone reaches this return through your Form W-2, box 12, code Q. They stay on this computer, in the working folder, and go into the filled form only.

          Trying other figures

Compute it again here to see what moves on the workbook's Computed return tab.

          Filing

          The filled forms are your return as computed here from the documents and answers on this page; every figure is checked against those forms before it is written, and the check's result is listed above. This page does not file a return. A paper return is signed and mailed to the address for your state in the Form 1040 instructions ("Where To File"); the figures can also be entered into e-file software of your choosing. Forms you received (W-2, 1099, W-2G) are not printed here; keep them with your records, and attach a W-2 to a mailed return as the instructions say.
